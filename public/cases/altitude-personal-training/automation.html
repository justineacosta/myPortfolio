What happens after someone presses Start coaching

Altitude’s site has no contact form. It has buttons: Start coaching opens a five-minute application, and Book a consult opens Elias’s calendar. It also makes promises a flow has to keep: a reply within two working days, nothing to sign until you have met your coach, and every Sunday check-in answered by Monday, by Elias himself. These are the flows behind all three. Pick a flow, then open any step to see what it does, what it reads and why it is there.
