From Book a chair to the next cut

Every trigger, flow, bot and agent behind Ostler’s site, wired as one graph: a chair booked in a barber’s own book, confirmed and reminded, a freed hour offered on, a visitor’s questions, a new member held to their barber’s forty, a shelf order picked by hand, the hour in the chair and the note that suggests the next one.

The client’s barber approves anything Claude drafts for a client’s inbox before it is sent. Templated confirmations, reminders, welcomes and order emails send on their own, and the Shop Q&A bot answers live, in the site chat only. Every payment is made on Stripe’s own pages, and any refund is made by a person, by hand. Reviews, The Ostler Letter, gift cards and the till are done by hand: no tool here reaches them.

PLAIN ENGLISH

Every term on the map, in one line.

Plain English for the technical words above.

n8n
The automation tool chair bookings, the Shop Q&A bot, both agents and the failed-run log run in.
Make
The automation tool new memberships run in, with a Router that sends each member one of two ways.
Zapier
The automation tool shelf orders run in, with Paths for a collection and a parcel.
Webhook
A web address Cal.com calls the moment a booking is made, moved or cancelled, which starts OS-01.
Claude
Anthropic’s AI model. It answers, plans and drafts in the bot and the two agents. None of the three flows uses it.
Cal.com
The booking tool where each barber keeps their own book, one account and one event type per service.
Stripe
Where members pay on a Payment Link and the bag is paid at checkout. Any refund is made there by hand.
Payment Link
A Stripe page for one barber’s £95 membership, which marks the subscription with that barber’s name.
Airtable
Where Ostler keeps Bookings, Members and Orders, written by the three flows and read by the agents.
Gmail
The shop’s own mailbox, which sends every template and every note a barber has approved.
Telegram
The chat app where the Front room group hears freed chairs and collections, barbers approve drafts, and Tomas hears of a failed run.
Front room
The shop’s Telegram group, which every barber is in, for freed chairs, new members and same-day collections.
Supabase
The database that stores the passages the Shop Q&A bot searches.
Google Sheets
Where the Failed runs and Agent runs tabs are kept, one row per failure or agent run.
Template
A fixed message with blanks for the name, barber, time and price. No AI writes it, so it may send alone.
Passage
One service, term or shelf item cut from the shop’s pages, as the search returns it.
Member’s cut
A member’s own visit, booked by their barber with no price, as membership covers it, like a Member’s tidy between cuts.
Standing
A member with a standing hour in their barber’s book, every three or four weeks.
Waiting list
The members who joined a barber who already had forty, and have no standing slot yet.
First refusal
A member’s benefit on Friday and Saturday evenings: their barber’s freed hour is offered to them first.
Free times
The open hours in a barber’s Cal.com book, read by the agents and never changed by them.
To pick
The Orders view the shop works as its pick list, until an order is Collected or Posted.
Collect today
Picking a shelf order up from 6 Carrow Mews the same day, offered until an hour before closing.
Agent runs
The Google Sheets tab that logs each agent run, with the barber’s yes.
Failed runs
The Google Sheets tab that logs each failed run from n8n, Make or Zapier, with no client’s details.
Approval gate
A hard stop. The client’s barber approves anything Claude drafts for a client’s inbox before it is sent.
Agent
An AI with one job that picks its own tools, one call at a time, until the job is done.
A worked example. Ostler is a fictional barbershop, not a client project, and figures are illustrative.Built by Justine Acosta
