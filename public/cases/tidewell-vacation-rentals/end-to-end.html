From an owner’s first request to the payout

Every trigger, flow, bot and agent behind Tidewell’s site, wired as one graph: an owner’s assessment, the walkthrough, guests and their questions, repairs, rates and reviews, and the statement that arrives by the tenth with payment the same day.

Owners approve every repair above $400, and a manager approves every rate change, every reply to a review and anything Claude drafts for an owner’s inbox.

PLAIN ENGLISH

Every term on the map, in one line.

Plain English for the technical words above.

n8n
The automation tool the assessment flow, the Guest help bot and both agents run in.
Make
The automation tool repair approval runs in, with a router that sends each job one of two ways.
Zapier
The automation tool the monthly statement runs in, with a review page for the manager.
Webhook
A web address the assessment form calls the moment it is sent, which starts TW-01.
Claude
Anthropic’s AI model. It outlines, drafts, answers and proposes here, and never sends an email on its own.
HubSpot
The CRM where Tidewell keeps every owner who asks for an assessment, and each assessment’s stage.
Airtable
Where Tidewell keeps its towns, managers, homes, repair jobs and statements.
Cal.com
The booking tool that holds each manager’s calendar and the hour for each walkthrough.
Gmail
The mailbox confirmations, repair requests and statements are sent from.
Telegram
The chat app where managers get briefs and approve drafts, the crew gets jobs, and the office hears what to pay.
Supabase
The database that stores the passages the Guest help bot searches.
Market
One of Tidewell’s three areas, the coast, the city or the mountains, each with its own managers.
Template
A fixed message with blanks for the date, time and manager. No AI writes it, so it may send alone.
Walkthrough
The hour a manager spends at the property before writing the projection.
Projection
The written twelve-month revenue projection the manager sends after the walkthrough.
Jobs form
The Airtable form a repair is logged on, with photos and a quote, which starts TW-02.
Management fee
Tidewell’s 22% of gross booking revenue, shown on every statement.
Review page
A page Zapier hosts where the manager approves a note or rejects it to rewrite.
Approval gate
A hard stop. No repair above $400, rate change, reply to a review or draft for an owner’s inbox goes on until the right person says yes.
Check-in guide
How arrival works at a Tidewell home, from the door code to check-out.
House rules
One set per home, like whether a dog may come.
Local guide
Where Tidewell’s staff eat, swim and take visiting family.
Passage
A rule or a step cut from a longer guide, as the search returns it.
Embed
Turn a piece of text into numbers that capture its meaning, so similar ideas can be found.
On-call person
The member of the team who answers urgent guest messages that night.
Agent
An AI with one job that picks its own tools, one call at a time, until the job is done.
Payout
What a statement says is paid to the owner, sent by a person the day the statement goes out.
A worked example. Tidewell is a fictional rental manager, not a client project, and figures are illustrative.Built by Justine Acosta
