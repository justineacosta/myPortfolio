PLAIN ENGLISH

Every term on the map, in one line.

Plain English for the technical words above.

n8n
The automation tool the viewing flow and both agents run in: triggers, branches and actions, wired as nodes.
Make
The automation tool the studio inbox runs in, with a router that sends each email down its own route.
Zapier
The automation tool the revision notice runs in, with a review page for the architect.
Webhook
A web address the form calls the moment it is sent, which starts the viewing flow.
Claude
Anthropic’s AI model. It qualifies, sorts, drafts and answers here, and never sends an email on its own.
HubSpot
The CRM where Oriel keeps every buyer, their viewings and what they have been told.
Airtable
Where the studio keeps its projects, the duty rota, the revisions and the care requests.
Cal.com
The booking tool that holds each architect’s calendar and the ninety-minute viewing slots.
Gmail
The studio’s mailbox, studio@oriel.pt, that confirmations, notes and follow-ups are sent from.
Telegram
The chat app where architects get briefs and approve drafts, and the founders hear from the press.
Supabase
The database that stores the passages the Q&A bot searches.
Template
A fixed message with blanks for the date, time and place. No AI writes it, so it may send alone.
Architect on duty
The architect who walks the Not sure yet viewings that week, and can open all four models.
Headset
The virtual reality headset a courier brings for a home viewing, with the model loaded.
Router
The step in Make that sends each email down the route for its kind.
Care request
What an owner asks after handover, opened for the project team in Airtable.
Model revision
A recorded change to a residence’s model, one row in the Revisions table.
Review page
A page Zapier hosts where the architect approves a note or rejects it to rewrite.
Approval gate
A hard stop. Nothing AI-written reaches a buyer’s inbox until the architect says yes.
Residence sheet
One document per residence: rooms, sizes, aspect, sun hours, materials and status.
Embed
Turn a piece of text into numbers that capture its meaning, so similar ideas can be found.
Passage
A room or a change cut from a longer document, as the search returns it.
Agent
An AI with one job that picks its own tools, one call at a time, until the job is done.
Handover
The day the owners get their keys, and the model of their home with them.
Owners’ model
The walkable model kept after handover: the record of what sits behind every wall.
A worked example. Oriel is a fictional developer, not a client project, and figures are illustrative.Built by Justine Acosta
