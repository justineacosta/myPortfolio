What happens after a buyer asks Westmere about a residence

Westmere’s site has no form. It ends on four buttons: Enquire on this residence, Book a private viewing, Request the full portfolio and Download the brochure. On the sample site they are a demonstration and send nothing; these flows are the build behind them once it is live. Enquire on this residence opens a short panel for a name, an email, an optional phone and a message, with the residence filled in from the page, and sends it to n8n. Book a private viewing opens Westmere’s Cal.com booking page, which offers Tuesday to Saturday only, by appointment, at the sales gallery, 9200 Sunset Blvd, Suite 40, and Cal.com tells Make. Request the full portfolio opens a panel like the enquiry’s and sends it to Zapier. Download the brochure starts no flow: it downloads the PDF at once and asks for nothing. Westmere is an Equal Housing Opportunity developer, DRE #02114471, so no step screens, sorts or scores a buyer on who they are. Claude works in the enquiry flow alone, and there it reads only what the buyer asked. A “qualified buyer” means proof of funds and nothing else, and only the sales director decides it, by hand. Pick a flow, then open any step to see what it does, what it reads and why it is there.
