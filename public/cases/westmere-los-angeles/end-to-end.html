From Enquire on this residence to a signed contract

Every trigger, flow, bot and agent behind Westmere’s site, wired as one graph: an enquiry read for what the buyer asked, the listing agent’s call within minutes, a private viewing booked Tuesday to Saturday, the hosting agent’s brief and follow-up, the full portfolio and the valuation pack after proof of funds, and the contract the sales team asks the Document Q&A bot about.

The hosting agent approves anything Claude drafts for a buyer’s inbox before it is sent. Westmere is an Equal Housing Opportunity developer, DRE #02114471, so no flow, bot or agent screens, scores, sorts or ranks a buyer, and none reads a protected trait or a budget. In WM-01 Claude sees only the message and the residence, and a fixed rule routes the result. A “qualified buyer” means proof of funds and nothing else, and only the sales director decides it, by hand. The brochure, the portfolio and the viewing confirmation are fixed templates and send on their own, and the Document Q&A bot answers the sales team only, never a buyer. Listing sites, offers and escrow are done by hand: no tool here reaches them.

PLAIN ENGLISH

Every term on the map, in one line.

Plain English for the technical words above.

n8n
The automation tool residence enquiries, the bot, both agents and the failed-run log run in.
Make
The automation tool private viewings run in, with a Router that runs its routes one after another.
Zapier
The automation tool portfolio requests run in, with Human in the Loop and a Filter.
Webhook
A web address another tool posts to: the enquiry panel to WM-01, Cal.com to Make and to the Viewing prep agent, and failed runs to WM-H02.
Claude
Anthropic’s AI model. It reads WM-01’s enquiries for three answers, and answers, plans and drafts in the bot and the two agents.
HubSpot
Where every buyer is a contact, with the residence they asked about, their enquiry and their next viewing.
Cal.com
Where buyers book a private viewing, Tuesday to Saturday, and which picks the hosting agent in turn.
Airtable
Where Westmere keeps its residences, its team, portfolio requests and documents.
Gmail
The private@westmere.la mailbox, which sends every template and every follow-up the hosting agent approved.
Telegram
The chat app where agents hear of enquiries and viewings, read briefs, approve drafts, and the sales team hears of a failed run.
Supabase
The database that stores the passages the Document Q&A bot and both agents search.
Google Sheets
Where the Failed runs and Agent runs tabs are kept.
Template
A fixed message with blanks for the name, the residence, the day or the host. No AI writes it.
Listing agent
The sales agent who holds a residence, alerted when a buyer asks to view or talk.
Hosting agent
The sales agent Cal.com picks in turn to host a private viewing at the sales gallery.
Sales director
The person who confirms proof of funds and files every contract.
Proof of funds
The only thing that makes a buyer qualified, checked by the sales director by hand and never stored.
Human in the Loop
Zapier’s approval step: the sales director gets an email link to a review page, and only their choice there counts.
Agent runs
The Google Sheets tab that logs each agent run, with the hosting agent’s yes.
Failed runs
The Google Sheets tab that logs each failed run from n8n, Make or Zapier, with no buyer’s details.
Approval gate
A hard stop. The hosting agent approves anything Claude drafts for a buyer’s inbox before it is sent.
Agent
An AI with one job that picks its own tools, one call at a time, until the job is done.
