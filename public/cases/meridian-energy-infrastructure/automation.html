What happens after a site survey request, a production fault or the end of the month at Meridian Energy Infrastructure

Meridian Energy Infrastructure designs, finances, builds and operates commercial solar and battery storage from Houston, San Antonio and Baton Rouge: 184 projects and 312 MW in TX, LA, MS, AL and OK. Request a Site Assessment opens the site survey wizard, which promises that an energy advisor will confirm a drone survey window within one business day. Once an asset runs, the site promises a contracted 48-hour response on any production fault, monthly production reporting measured at the meter and reconciled to utility settlement, and a guarantee: if the asset underperforms the model, we write the cheque. These three flows keep those promises: site surveys on Make, production faults on n8n and production reports on Zapier. People make the calls. An energy advisor sees every survey request. The duty lead in the operations centre dispatches every technician, by phone; no flow dispatches anyone. The asset manager approves every report, and decides any payment under the guarantee by hand; no client email names a payment or an amount. No step tells a client to touch, reset or isolate any equipment, and an emergency goes to 911 and the utility. Claude only scores survey requests, and the score goes to the sheet and the advisors, never to the visitor; the Report note agent, planned on the AI agents page, drafts each report’s note for the asset manager. Strong sites get a survey on the calendar; the rest get a useful guide instead of silence. An energy advisor sees every request at once: a strong site’s survey window is confirmed within one business day, as the wizard promises, and for the rest an advisor is in touch within one business day.

Every production fault the operations centre logs opens a ticket within a minute, the duty lead hears at once, and the client gets a notice that Meridian responds within 48 hours. A person dispatches the technician, and the flow warns the operations centre before the 48 hours run out.
