From a question in the site chat to a month of generation reported against the model

Every trigger, flow, bot and agent behind Meridian Energy Infrastructure’s site, wired as one graph: a visitor’s question answered from the site’s own published figures; a survey request from the wizard scored, logged and in front of an energy advisor within minutes, and answered by a person within one business day; a strong-fit firm researched from its public pages and sent a first email an advisor approved; a production fault logged by the operations centre, ticketed, announced to the client with the contracted 48-hour response and dispatched by the duty lead; and each month, every asset’s report, measured at the meter and reconciled to utility settlement, with a note the asset manager approved.

No flow, bot or agent tells a client to touch, reset, switch or isolate any equipment, and none reads SCADA or dispatches a technician: the operations centre logs every fault by hand, and the duty lead dispatches by phone. Every fault notice, and the bot’s fixed reply, says to leave the equipment as it is, and if there is fire, smoke or damaged equipment, or anyone is hurt, to keep clear and call 911, then the utility. If the asset underperforms the model, Meridian Energy Infrastructure writes the cheque: whether a payment is due, and how much, is the asset manager’s decision, made by hand. No flow, bot or agent works out, proposes or promises a saving for a site, a production figure or a payment, and no client email or note names a payment or an amount. An energy advisor approves every first email Claude drafts for a prospect’s inbox, and the asset manager approves every report note before the report reaches a client. That is every Lead research email, in Telegram, and every report note, in Airtable. The booking email, the savings guide, the fault notice and the report email are fixed templates; the report email sends only once the asset manager has approved the report. The bot answers live, without a yes, from the site’s pages only. In ME-01, ME-02 and ME-03 Claude only scores survey requests; the two agents run beside the flows, and the Lead research agent reads ME-01’s score rather than scoring again.

PLAIN ENGLISH

Every term on the map, in one line.

Plain English for the technical words above.

Make
The automation tool survey requests run in, from the wizard to the booking email or the guide.
n8n
The automation tool production faults, the bot, both agents and the failed-run log run in.
Zapier
The automation tool monthly production reports run in.
Claude
Anthropic’s AI model. It scores survey requests, answers in the bot, drafts the Lead research email and the report note.
Airtable
Where Meridian Energy Infrastructure keeps its assets, faults, tickets, production months and reports.
Google Sheets
Where the pipeline sheet of survey requests, the bot’s published figures and the Failed runs tab are kept.
Cal.com
Where a strong site books its drone survey window, for an energy advisor to confirm.
Gmail
The mailbox that sends every booking email, guide, fault notice, report and approved first email.
Telegram
The chat app where the advisors, the duty leads and the asset managers hear of their work, and advisors approve first emails.
Supabase
The database that stores the bot’s passages and the CRM of strong-fit requests and their research.
Wizard
The site’s four questions behind Request a Site Assessment, ending in Book the site survey.
Strong fit
ME-01’s route for a site scoring 7 or more out of 10.
Router
Make’s way of splitting a scenario; its fallback route takes a low score or no score.
Energy advisor
The person who confirms every survey window, follows up every other request and approves every first email.
Duty lead
The person in the operations centre who dispatches a technician by phone.
Asset manager
The person accountable for an operating asset, who approves its report and decides any payment.
SCADA
The operations centre’s 24/7 monitoring. People read it; no flow does.
Respond by
The time on a ticket 48 hours after the fault was detected: the contracted response, not a repair.
Note draft
The field where the Report note agent writes. The asset manager edits it into the note the client reads.
Shortfall decision
The asset manager’s own call, by hand, on any payment for a month below the model.
Template
A fixed message with blanks for the site, the asset or the month. No AI writes it.
Failed runs
The Google Sheets tab that logs each failed or halted run from Make, n8n or Zapier.
Approval gate
A hard stop. An energy advisor approves every first email Claude drafts for a prospect’s inbox, and the asset manager approves every report note before the report reaches a client.
Agent
An AI with one job that reads what it needs, one call at a time, until the job is done.
