A bot that quotes only the site’s own figures, and two agents that never write the cheque

Facility and finance teams ask Meridian Energy Infrastructure the same things before they request a site assessment: what solar could save, how big a site has to be, how the 25 years are structured. This is the bot that answers them in the site chat from the company’s own published figures, the agent that researches each strong-fit request for an energy advisor, the agent that drafts the note on each monthly production report, and the two patterns underneath all three. No bot or agent here tells anyone to touch, reset, switch or isolate any equipment, or gives an electrical step of any kind: the operations centre and the field technicians do that work, and the duty lead dispatches every technician by phone. Anything about a fault, a site’s equipment or an emergency gets one fixed answer: leave the equipment as it is, and if there is fire, smoke or damaged equipment, or anyone is hurt, keep clear and call 911, then your utility. No bot or agent works out, proposes or promises a saving for a visitor’s own site, a production figure or a payment. The bot quotes only the ranges the site publishes, with the source. The Report note agent never states, works out or promises a shortfall payment or an amount: if the asset underperforms the model, Meridian Energy Infrastructure writes the cheque, and whether a payment is due, and how much, is the asset manager’s decision, made by hand. An energy advisor approves every first email Claude drafts for a prospect’s inbox, and the asset manager approves every report note before the report reaches a client. The bot answers live, without a yes, from the site’s pages only and always with the source. Neither agent is a step in ME-01, ME-02 or ME-03: the Lead research agent reads ME-01’s score and runs beside it, and the Report note agent writes to the report’s row beside ME-03, so in the three flows Claude still only scores survey requests.

AT A GLANCE

Two patterns, two rules

Every build below is one of two patterns. None tells anyone to touch equipment, and none puts a number on a site or a payment. An energy advisor approves every first email Claude drafts for a prospect’s inbox, and the asset manager approves every report note before the report reaches a client.

  • 1

    RAG chatbots

    The Website sales bot searches the site’s own pages and published figures, then answers in the site chat with the source, and offers a site assessment. It never puts a number on a visitor’s own site.

  • 2

    AI agents

    Lead research reads ME-01’s score, researches the firm from public pages and drafts an energy advisor’s first email. Report note drafts the plain-language note on each monthly report. Neither touches a payment.

  • 3

    A person’s yes

    An energy advisor approves every first email Claude drafts for a prospect’s inbox, and the asset manager approves every report note before the report reaches a client. The bot answers live from the site, so it needs no yes.

HOW THE WEBSITE SALES BOT WORKS

Search the site first, then answer

The site’s own pages and the figures they publish are prepared once. Every question then meets a fixed check for equipment words, and only then searches the pages before Claude says a word. Click any step.

HOW AN AGENT WORKS

Plan, use a tool, check, repeat

A strong-fit request or a drafted report starts it. Claude reads one thing at a time, a fixed check reads the draft, and a person says yes. Click any step.

THREE SAMPLE BUILDS

Three builds for Meridian Energy Infrastructure

One bot for visitors on the site, one agent for the energy advisors and one for the asset managers. Show any one’s route on its diagram.

RAG chatbots

  • SAMPLE BUILDRAG chatbot

    Website sales bot

    For Meridian Energy Infrastructure · commercial solar

    Answers Meridian Energy Infrastructure visitors’ cost and fit questions, then offers a site assessment. It answers from the site’s own pages and the figures they publish, always with the source, and quotes a figure only as the site publishes it: it never works out a saving or a production figure for a visitor’s own site, and never promises a payment. A fixed n8n check sends any question about a fault, equipment or an emergency to a fixed reply before Claude sees it: “If you see fire, smoke or damaged equipment, or anyone is hurt, keep clear and call 911, then your utility. Don’t touch, reset or switch off any equipment. For anything else, call an energy advisor on (713) 555-0142.” The assessment it offers is the site’s own wizard, the path ME-01 runs, and it never calls it free. Anything the site does not say goes to an energy advisor.

    Asked
    What would rooftop solar save on our distribution warehouse?
    Answer
    Typically 18 – 27% below blended retail rate under a power purchase agreement (Solutions guide). Book a site assessment?
    Tools: Claude, Supabase, n8n, Cal.com

AI agents

  • SAMPLE BUILDAI agent

    Lead research agent

    For Meridian Energy Infrastructure · commercial solar

    Reads each firm asking Meridian Energy Infrastructure for an assessment, scores the fit, drafts a reply. The fit score is ME-01’s own: when ME-01 logs a request as Strong fit, a separate n8n workflow reads that row’s 1–10 score and reason, and never scores again. It reads only public pages about the firm, never about the person, and saves that research, with its sources, in Supabase, the CRM, holding nothing about the requester beyond what they gave in the wizard. Then Claude drafts the energy advisor’s first personal email: no figure for the site, no saving, no payment, and never the score or the word free. An energy advisor approves it in Telegram before it sends. ME-01’s own emails stay fixed templates, so in ME-01 Claude only scores.

    Trigger
    New assessment request from a 300,000 sq ft cold-storage site.
    Result
    Fit 8 of 10, saved to the CRM, first email drafted for your yes. The 8 is ME-01’s score, read from the Requests row; the yes is an energy advisor’s, in Telegram.
    Tools: Claude, n8n, Supabase, Telegram
  • SAMPLE BUILDAI agent

    Report note agent

    For Meridian Energy Infrastructure · commercial solar

    Drafts the plain-language note on each monthly production report ME-03 drafts, for the asset manager to approve. It writes only from the report’s own figures, generation measured at the meter and reconciled to utility settlement, against the model, and adds no cause it was not given. It never states, works out or promises a shortfall payment or an amount: any payment is the asset manager’s decision, made by hand. A fixed check turns a draft with a figure not on the report, or a word about money or equipment, into no draft, and a report whose Note draft is still empty when the asset manager opens it is one they write by hand. It writes Note draft on the report’s row and never sends: the asset manager edits it into Note and sets Approved, and ME-03 sends the report.

    Trigger
    ME-03 drafts an asset’s May report: 360,000 kWh metered against 400,000 modelled.
    Result
    In Note draft: “Generation in May was 360,000 kWh, measured at the meter and reconciled to utility settlement, 10% below the model’s 400,000 kWh.” No cause, no payment, no amount. The asset manager edits it and approves.
    Tools: Claude, n8n, Airtable
GUARDRAILS

Four rules every build follows

They are what keep a solar developer’s bot and agents away from its equipment, its measured numbers and its cheques, which belong to people.

  • G1

    Never an equipment step

    No bot or agent here tells anyone to touch, reset, switch or isolate any equipment, or gives an electrical step of any kind: the operations centre and the field technicians do that work, and the duty lead dispatches every technician by phone. Anything about a fault, a site’s equipment or an emergency gets one fixed answer: leave the equipment as it is, and if there is fire, smoke or damaged equipment, or anyone is hurt, keep clear and call 911, then your utility. The bot sends any question with an equipment word to that fixed reply before Claude sees it, and the fixed check on every agent draft turns one that names a step on equipment into no draft. No build reads SCADA, a meter or a fault, or dispatches anyone.

  • G2

    Published ranges, never a promise

    No bot or agent works out, proposes or promises a saving for a visitor’s own site, a production figure or a payment. The bot quotes only the ranges the site publishes, with the source. The Report note agent never states, works out or promises a shortfall payment or an amount: if the asset underperforms the model, Meridian Energy Infrastructure writes the cheque, and whether a payment is due, and how much, is the asset manager’s decision, made by hand. The bot’s example answer is the site’s own range, with its source, and a number for a visitor’s own site comes from the survey: “a measured number, not a sales estimate.” Reports has no field for an amount, and the note agent never reads Guarantee review.

  • G3

    A person says yes

    An energy advisor approves every first email Claude drafts for a prospect’s inbox, and the asset manager approves every report note before the report reaches a client. The first email waits in the energy advisors’ Telegram group, and only the form’s submit records a yes. The note waits on the report’s row, and only the asset manager can set a report Approved. The bot answers live from the site’s pages, so it needs no yes: it is chat only, sends no email and books nothing, and a question the site does not answer goes to an energy advisor.

  • G4

    The firm’s public facts only

    The Lead research agent reads only public pages about the organisation, never about the person, and keeps nothing about the requester beyond the name, organisation and email they gave in the wizard. The agent never scores a site: it reads ME-01’s score from the row. The bot keeps no chat, and asks for no name, email or bill.

PLAIN ENGLISH

Every term on this page, in one line

Plain English for the technical words above.

RAG
Retrieval-augmented generation: search the site’s own pages first, then answer from what was found.
Solutions guide
The site’s Why Solar, Solutions and Process pages, as the bot cites them.
Published figures
The ranges the site publishes, like 18 – 27% below blended retail rate, one row each in a Google Sheet.
Passage
One section of a page or one sheet row, as the search returns it and the answer cites it.
Embedding
A list of numbers that captures what a piece of text means, so similar ideas sit close together.
Vector search
Finding the stored passages whose embeddings sit closest to the question’s.
pgvector
A Postgres add-on that stores embeddings and runs vector search, here inside Supabase.
Equipment words
The fixed list the operations centre keeps, like breaker, reset or smoke. A question with one gets the fixed reply, never Claude.
Hand-off
Pointing the visitor to an energy advisor, by phone or by email. The bot forwards nothing.
Site assessment
The site survey the wizard books: drone LIDAR or borings, a structural review and a 25-year production model.
Agent
An AI that takes a goal, picks its own tools and works step by step until the job is done.
Trigger
The event that starts a run: a strong-fit request logged, or a report drafted.
Tool call
One action an agent asks for, like reading the firm’s website or saving the research in Supabase.
Fit score
ME-01’s 1–10 score for a site, with one line on why. The Lead research agent reads it and never scores again.
CRM
The Supabase table of strong-fit requests, their research and their first emails.
Note draft
The field on a report’s row where the Report note agent writes. The asset manager edits it into Note.
Fixed check
An n8n Code node that reads every draft for figures, money words and equipment words, behind Claude.
Loop-back
A cable back to an earlier step: the agent reads again, or redrafts an email the advisor sent back.
Approval gate
A point where the run stops until a person says yes. An energy advisor approves every first email Claude drafts for a prospect’s inbox, and the asset manager approves every report note before the report reaches a client.
Production guarantee
The site’s promise: if the asset underperforms the model, Meridian Energy Infrastructure writes the cheque. The asset manager decides any payment.
Energy advisor
The person who answers survey requests, confirms survey windows and approves every first email.
Asset manager
The person accountable for an operating asset, who approves its monthly report and decides any payment.
Claude
Anthropic’s AI model. It answers in the bot, researches and drafts in Lead research, and drafts the report note.
n8n
The workflow tool that runs the bot and both agents: it listens for triggers and makes the tool calls.
Supabase
A hosted Postgres database: the bot’s store of passages, and the CRM the Lead research agent saves to.
Cal.com
Where a strong site books its drone survey window through ME-01, for an energy advisor to confirm.
Airtable
Where Meridian Energy Infrastructure keeps its assets, faults, tickets, production months and reports.
Telegram
The chat app where the energy advisors approve first emails.
