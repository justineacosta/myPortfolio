From Get a free quote to the last box

Every trigger, flow, bot and agent behind Harbor & Pine’s site, wired as one graph: a question answered from the pricing guide, an estimate request priced by a coordinator the same working day, a walkthrough prepared, a move booked by hand, the day-before message, the crew on the day, the invoice and its reminder, and a building’s certificate of insurance.

A coordinator approves anything Claude drafts for a customer’s inbox before it is sent. The site promises “Same-day written reply, no automated pricing bots”, so Claude’s range goes to the coordinators only, and the Help-desk RAG bot answers live, in the site chat only, never with a price. The move-day message is a fixed template and sends on its own, and a certificate reaches a building only after its coordinator checks it. Invoices are raised by hand, and a person handles every refund and damage claim. Ferries, cranes, crating and reviews are done by hand: no tool here reaches them.

PLAIN ENGLISH

Every term on the map, in one line.

Plain English for the technical words above.

n8n
The automation tool estimate requests, the bot, both agents and the failed-run log run in.
Make
The automation tool the move-day message runs in, with a Router that sends each move one of two ways.
Zapier
The automation tool certificates of insurance run in, with a Filter and Human in the Loop.
Webhook
A web address another tool posts to: the quote form to HP-01, Stripe’s invoice.overdue to the Billing follow-up agent, and failed Make and Zapier steps to HP-H02.
Claude
Anthropic’s AI model. It drafts HP-01’s range for the coordinators only, and answers, plans and drafts in the bot and the two agents.
HubSpot
Where every estimate request is a deal in the Moves pipeline, owned by the coordinator who answered.
Cal.com
Where customers book an on-site or video walkthrough with a coordinator.
Airtable
Where Harbor & Pine keeps booked moves, the team and certificate requests.
Stripe
Where the coordinator raises each invoice by hand after the move, and where the customer pays it.
Gmail
The office mailbox, hello@harborandpine.example, which sends every template and every reminder a coordinator approved.
Telegram
The chat app where the coordinators’ group sees each request, approves drafts, reads walkthrough briefs and hears of a failed run.
Supabase
The database that stores the passages the Help-desk RAG bot and the Walkthrough prep agent search.
Google Sheets
Where the rate card, the Failed runs tab and the Agent runs tab are kept.
Rate card
The coordinators’ own sheet of crew, truck, mileage, packing and specialty rates, kept by Marisol. Only HP-01 reads it.
Template
A fixed message with blanks for the name, the coordinator, the time or the range a coordinator approved. No AI writes it.
Passage
One section of the pricing guide or one FAQ answer, as the search returns it.
Walkthrough
A coordinator counting rooms, stairs, access and parking, on site within 20 miles of Bellhaven or over video.
Lead mover
The person who walks the house before anything is lifted and leads the crew on the day. The customer has their number.
Human in the Loop
Zapier’s approval step: the coordinator gets an email link to a review page, and only their choice there counts.
Agent runs
The Google Sheets tab that logs each agent run, with the coordinator’s yes.
Failed runs
The Google Sheets tab that logs each failed or halted run from n8n, Make or Zapier, with no customer’s details.
Approval gate
A hard stop. A coordinator approves anything Claude drafts for a customer’s inbox before it is sent.
Agent
An AI with one job that picks its own tools, one call at a time, until the job is done.
