What happens after someone books an evaluation at Range

Range’s site has no form. It has buttons: Schedule your evaluation and Reserve open Mara’s Cal.com calendar, and Start a plan opens Stripe. It also makes promises a flow has to keep: Mara will be in touch before your visit, a superbill comes after each visit, and the six-visit plan is used within four months. One rule sits above all three flows: clinical content never enters them, or Claude, HubSpot or Airtable. They carry scheduling and billing data only, and clinical notes stay in Mara’s own notes system. Mara approves anything Claude drafts for a patient’s inbox, and none of these flows uses Claude at all: every email is a fixed template.
