From Schedule your evaluation to the last plan visit

Every trigger, flow, bot and agent behind Range’s site, wired as one graph: a booked evaluation and a visitor’s questions, the visit itself, the superbill after it, the six-visit plan and its reminders, and the inbox, sorted so anything clinical reaches Mara untouched.

Mara approves anything Claude drafts for a patient’s inbox, and makes any refund or change to a plan herself. Nothing clinical is stored, summarised or forwarded by Claude, Airtable or any flow.

PLAIN ENGLISH

Every term on the map, in one line.

Plain English for the technical words above.

n8n
The automation tool the evaluation flow and both agents run in. It saves no executions of any of them.
Make
The automation tool the superbill runs in: it hears a paid visit and drafts the superbill.
Zapier
The automation tool the six-visit plan runs in, as three Zaps.
Execution
n8n’s saved copy of a run. Range’s n8n workflows save none, so no booking or email is kept there.
Claude
Anthropic’s AI model. It answers, sorts and drafts here, and never writes a superbill, a reminder or the intake email.
Airtable
Where Range keeps its patients, visits and plans, with scheduling and billing fields only.
Stripe
Where a patient pays for a visit or a plan, on Stripe’s own page.
Payment Link
A Stripe page for one price. Mara opens a visit’s link for the patient at the end of the visit.
Cal.com
The booking tool that holds Mara’s calendar, the evaluation, follow-ups and the free call.
Gmail
Range’s mailbox at hello@rangept.co, where templates, superbill drafts and approved replies come from.
Telegram
The chat app where Mara gets alerts and pings, and approves drafts.
Supabase
The database that stores the public passages the Clinic Q&A bot searches.
Passage
A price or a practical answer cut from one of Range’s pages, as the search returns it.
Template
A fixed message with blanks for the name, time and price. No AI writes it, so it may send alone.
Edit Fields
The n8n step that keeps the scheduling fields of a booking and drops everything else.
Superbill
The receipt a patient sends for out-of-network benefits. Mara adds its codes from her notes and sends it herself.
Six-visit plan
Six follow-ups for $1,020, $170 each, used within four months and shareable within a household.
Notes system
Mara’s own clinic notes system, where everything clinical lives. No flow, agent or Claude reads or writes it.
Free 15-minute call
A short call with Mara, booked on Cal.com, where anything clinical is talked through with her.
For Mara
The Gmail label for anything clinical or unsure. The email is left untouched for Mara to read.
Admin
The Gmail label for a practical email, like a visit to move, which gets a reply drafted from the site’s facts.
Business associate agreement
A signed contract with a vendor that handles patient information. Before any real patient’s details go into these tools, Range would need a signed business associate agreement with each vendor that handles them, and would replace any vendor that won’t sign one.
Agent runs
The Google Sheets tab that logs each agent run: the time, the label and Mara’s yes, never an email’s words.
Approval gate
A hard stop. No reply Claude drafts reaches a patient until Mara says yes.
Agent
An AI with one job that picks its own tools, one call at a time, until the job is done.
