What happens after someone asks Riverdale & Brooks for a consultation

The Riverdale & Brooks site has one form, Request a consultation: a full name, phone, email, the area of law and, if they like, a few sentences about their situation. It says sending it creates no attorney-client relationship, and asks people to leave confidential details out. One rule sits above all three flows: nothing confidential is stored in Claude, Airtable, Telegram or any flow. They carry names, contact details, the area of law and scheduling or billing data only. The optional few sentences pass through n8n and Gmail only to reach Daniel’s own inbox, and are saved nowhere else. The conflict check is done by hand, before Ruth confirms any consultation, and what a matter is about stays with Daniel. Ruth approves any reply Claude drafts before it reaches anyone’s inbox, Daniel approves every monthly invoice before it goes out, and none of these flows uses Claude at all: every email is a fixed template.
