From Send request to “where your matter stands”

Every trigger, flow, bot and agent behind the firm’s site, wired as one graph: a consultation request and a visitor’s questions, the office inbox, the conflict check done by hand before each consultation is confirmed, the prep for it, the engagement letter, the monthly invoice and the notice at every milestone. Mail from a client, a court or opposing counsel reaches Daniel untouched.

Ruth approves any reply Claude drafts before it reaches anyone’s inbox, Daniel approves every monthly invoice before it goes out. Templated replies and notices send on their own, and the bot answers live, in the site chat only. Nothing confidential is stored in Claude, Airtable, Telegram or any flow: the few sentences from the form pass through n8n and Gmail only to reach Daniel’s inbox, and the conflict check is done by hand.

PLAIN ENGLISH

Every term on the map, in one line.

Plain English for the technical words above.

n8n
The automation tool the consultation request and both agents run in. It keeps a run only while it waits for Ruth’s yes, and none once it ends.
Make
The automation tool the monthly invoice runs in: it gathers the time and drafts each invoice in Stripe.
Zapier
The automation tool the milestone notice runs in, as a notice Zap and a Monday reminder Zap.
Execution
n8n’s copy of a run. RB-01 and the prep agent save none. An inbox run is held only while it waits for Ruth’s yes, and none is kept once it ends.
Claude
Anthropic’s AI model. It answers, sorts and drafts here, and drafts the New enquiry and Admin replies for Ruth’s yes. It never writes the fixed consultation replies, an invoice or a notice.
Airtable
Where the firm keeps contacts, matters and time entries, with contact, area, scheduling and billing fields only.
Stripe
Where each invoice is drafted, sent by Daniel and paid online, or marked paid by Ruth for a check.
Cal.com
The booking tool that holds the two 30-minute consultation types, in the office or by video.
Gmail
The firm’s two mailboxes: office@, where templates and approved replies come from, and Daniel’s own.
office@
The firm’s shared mailbox, office@riverdalebrooks.law, the only one the inbox triage agent sorts.
Telegram
The chat app where Ruth and Daniel get alerts, pings and the prep, and Ruth approves drafts.
Supabase
The database that stores the public passages the Practice Q&A bot and both agents search.
Passage
A fee or a practical answer cut from one of the firm’s pages or guides, as the search returns it.
Template
A fixed message with blanks for the name, area and fee. No AI writes it, so it may send alone.
Edit Fields
The n8n step that keeps the contact details and the area of a request and drops the few sentences.
Few sentences
The optional box on the form. They pass through n8n and Gmail only to reach Daniel’s inbox, and no table, log or Claude keeps them.
Legal advice
Advice on someone’s own situation. Daniel gives it to his clients; the bot and the agents never do.
Conflict check
Making sure the firm acts for no one on the other side. Ruth runs it by hand before she confirms a consultation, never a flow.
Booking request
A Cal.com booking with Requires confirmation on: it waits for Ruth to confirm it, after the conflict check.
Engagement letter
The letter, signed by both, that sets the plan and the fee. Only then does an attorney-client relationship begin.
For Daniel
The Gmail label for mail from a client, a court or opposing counsel, an attachment, or anything confidential or unsure. It is left untouched, with no draft.
New enquiry
The Gmail label for someone asking to meet, who gets a reply with the right fee and a link to request a time.
Admin
The Gmail label for a practical question, like parking or the hours, which gets a reply drafted from the site.
Court domains
An Airtable list of the courts’ public email domains. n8n sends their mail For Daniel before Claude sees it. No client data.
Agent runs
The Google Sheets tab that logs each agent run: the time, whether an email or a booking started it, each tool call, and any label or yes, never an email’s words.
Confidentiality terms
A vendor’s written terms on how it keeps what it is given. Before any client’s matter goes into these tools, the firm would confirm each vendor’s confidentiality terms and the client’s informed consent, as the profession’s ethics guidance on AI expects.
Approval gate
A hard stop. Ruth approves any reply Claude drafts before it reaches anyone’s inbox, Daniel approves every monthly invoice before it goes out.
Agent
An AI with one job that picks its own tools, one call at a time, until the job is done.
